ALTER EGO AutoPost

ALTER EGO AutoPost is the private publishing tool we use to post our own content to our own accounts. For TikTok, it connects to the ALTER EGO account through Login with TikTok and the Content Posting API. Before anything goes to TikTok, the account owner reviews each video on an approval page: they preview the video, edit the title, choose who can see it, set comment, Duet and Stitch options, declare any commercial content, and agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Only approved videos are uploaded, at the scheduled time.

The tool is not offered to the public and only posts to accounts owned by ALTER EGO. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
