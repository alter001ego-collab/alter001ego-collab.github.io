Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information ALTER EGO AutoPost ("the tool", "we") handles. The tool is a private publishing tool that ALTER EGO uses to post its own content to its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • TikTok: when the account owner signs in with TikTok, we receive the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name, username and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos. We use this only to show which account will receive a post and to publish the videos the owner approves.
  • Facebook, Instagram and YouTube: access tokens for the ALTER EGO Page and channels, used only to publish ALTER EGO's own posts and first comments.
  • Content: the videos, images and captions ALTER EGO creates for posting.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok, Meta or Google users. We do not read messages, contacts, analytics of other accounts, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How information is stored

Access tokens and content are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by ALTER EGO. Information is sent only to the platform being posted to (TikTok, Meta or Google), through their official APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Content is shared only with the platform it is published to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool. You can revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → ALTER EGO AutoPost → Remove access.
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business integrations → remove ALTER EGO AutoPost.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove ALTER EGO AutoPost.

Removing access, or deleting the tool's local files, deletes all stored tokens. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the ALTER EGO account owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
